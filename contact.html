Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How far in advance can I book a room?

You can book a room up to 30 days in advance. For large groups or special events, please contact us directly as we may be able to accommodate further in advance.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel your booking free of charge up to 24 hours before your scheduled time. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a fee.

Can I bring my own food or drinks?

Outside food and drinks are not permitted. We have a full menu of delicious food and beverages available for order during your session.

Do you have options for large groups?

Yes! Our VIP Suite can accommodate up to 15 people. For even larger groups, we can book multiple adjacent rooms. Contact us for special arrangements.

Is there an age restriction?

During daytime hours, all ages are welcome. After 8pm, we require guests to be 18+ with valid ID. Alcohol service is strictly for those 21+ with valid ID.
