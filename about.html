About Diva Karaoke

Creating unforgettable karaoke experiences since 2015

Our Story

Diva karaoke was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to create the ultimate entertainment experience where friends and family could gather to sing their hearts out while enjoying delicious food and drinks.

What started as a small karaoke bar with just three private rooms has grown into the city's premier karaoke destination, featuring state-of-the-art sound systems, an extensive song library, and a menu crafted by our talented chefs.

We believe that great memories are made when good food, great music, and wonderful company come together. That's why we've created an environment where you can let loose, be yourself, and create moments you'll cherish forever.
